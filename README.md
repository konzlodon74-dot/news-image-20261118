An entry for the make.fun bounty **"Sum up 18 November's world news in one
image"** — the bounty page is
[make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261118](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261118).

`main.py` (Python, standard library only) validates the front-page image
`entry.png` against the bounty gate — PNG/JPEG, at most 1 MiB, at most
1024×1024, whole-file chunk/CRC checks — and writes it to
`/output/entry.png`. Run it with:

```
python3 main.py            # writes /output/entry.png
python3 main.py out.png    # or write somewhere else
```

## What's in the picture

One newspaper front page for **Wednesday 18 November 2026**, six stories
across regions and fields:

- **Middle East — U.S. strikes an Iranian missile site, Iran hits back.**
  U.S. jets hit a Revolutionary Guard launch site on Qeshm Island; Iran
  answered with drones against U.S. bases in Kuwait and Bahrain.
- **Asia — Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen.** The two leaders opened the
  APEC summit a day before their three-way meeting with President Trump.
- **Science — Voyager 1 reaches one light-day from Earth**, crossing at
  5:16 a.m. ET, about 16 billion miles out.
- **US politics — Peltola wins Alaska.** Democrat Mary Peltola's win in
  the last Senate race called gives Democrats a 56–44 majority.
- **Europe — Ukraine hits the Crimea bridge again**, a second day of
  strikes on the Kerch Bridge; part of the road span collapsed.
- **Health — the U.S. loses its measles-free status** after 26 years,
  with 5,412 cases recorded in 2026, PAHO confirmed.

Stories as reported on 18 November 2026 by Reuters, AP, AFP, the BBC,
NASA/JPL and PAHO coverage.
